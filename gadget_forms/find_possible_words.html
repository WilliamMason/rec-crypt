Find possible words in a null cipher

  1. Paste the null cipher into the input area and click Initialize. The numbered ciphertext words appear below the input.
  2. Click a numbered word to start the search there, or type the start word number. Enter -1 to search from every start word in turn.
  3. Set the minimum and maximum number of consecutive ciphertext words a plaintext word may span.
  4. Choose how many letters each ciphertext word may supply (1 or 2). Letters taken from one word must appear in it in forward order.
  5. Choose the offset position that letter positions are counted from: the beginning (1, 2, 3, ...), the end (1 = last letter), or the middle (0 = middle letter, -1 before it, 1 after it). Middle is offered only when every ciphertext word has an odd number of letters.
  6. Optionally, list only words containing a letter sequence (e.g. "ten"), use a custom word list file, or add extra words to the word list.
  7. Click Find words. Each hit shows which letters came from which ciphertext word and at which positions, followed by the letter position, counted from the chosen offset, that is shared by the most words (e.g. "first letter in 3/4 words"). Hits with the most consistent positions are listed first.
